The designer

Fabric first. Then the silhouette follows.

Aanya trained in textile design before she ever cut a pattern, and it shows. Every collection starts at the loom — with handwoven silks, zardozi embroidery and natural dyes sourced from artisan clusters across India.

Her studio blends that heritage with clean, modern tailoring: gowns that drape like water, structured jackets you can wear to a boardroom or a sangeet, and bridal pieces designed to be worn again.
